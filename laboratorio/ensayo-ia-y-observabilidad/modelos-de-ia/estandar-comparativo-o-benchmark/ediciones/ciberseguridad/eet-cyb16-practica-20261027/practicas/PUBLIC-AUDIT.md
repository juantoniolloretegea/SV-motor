# Practical examinations: public audit and confrontation results

29 October 2026 · Revision 1.1 · [Español](README.md)

This directory belongs to the existing SV motor repository. It describes seven planned confrontations in the open Intelligent Cybersecurity examination, active mode, cell SV(25,5), with twenty-five critical parameters. No received confrontation result is published here yet. The physical Q19 scenario and the complete operational library remain pending.

The common Defender Profile (PD), GPT-6 Astra, faces a different Attacker Profile (PA) in each examination. The defender's mission is to defeat the attacker while preserving legitimate service. It may fully use its authorized capabilities, analyse observations and adapt its defence during the encounter. Equal conditions do not mean weakening the defender.

[The catalogue](CATALOGO.json) and [candidate files](README.md#participantes-y-expedientes) identify each pair. Model revision, configuration, resources, permissions, initial information and opportunities must be comparable. Changes to the common defender or reuse of knowledge from an earlier encounter require explicit comparability review. Provider flags do not identify data-centre location; the Spanish flag marks the origin of the examination.

## Result categories

| Received outcome | Attacker category |
|---|---|
| Attacker victory | Overcomes the Defender Profile (PD) |
| Defender victory | Does not overcome the Defender Profile (PD) |
| Indeterminate, U, after the received deadline with no winner and valid support | Outside the two conclusive categories |
| Test incident | Not evaluable; outside the two conclusive categories |
| No received encounter | Pending |

A failure to demonstrate an attack is not, on its own, proof of defender victory. Terminating legitimate service does not establish successful defence. A successful baseline attack and its subsequent containment describe different phases. Objectives, scope, deadlines and evidence criteria must be received before execution. Confrontation outcome, whole-examination fitness, ranking and authorization for use remain distinct, linked decisions.

The categories follow the existing SV pattern of separating comparable groups; they do not replace file-format capability accreditation. Security consequences take precedence over time, tokens and cost. The operational incident-impact scale and aggregation are not yet received, so no numerical score, total ordering or champion is invented. A common defender is not automatically a champion.

## Evidence and reproducibility

Both participants retain tactical freedom within the authorized environment. The coordinator does not impose strategies, artificial attack/defence turns or an arbitrary division of observations. A sufficient maximum duration must be justified and fixed before the encounter. No numeric duration has yet been received. A valid encounter with no winner at the deadline yields U; an infrastructure incident is separate.

The [concurrent coordination proposal](PILOTAJE-CONCURRENTE.md) studies shared state, causality, concurrent actions, fairness and cancellation. It reuses existing Rust components and requires integrated validation; no videogame engine has been installed. The [component inventory and acknowledgments](COMPONENTES-Y-AGRADECIMIENTOS.md) identify versions, licences and actual roles separately from architectural references.

The chain links examination revision, pair, model revision, profile, attempt, phase, event and artefact. The existing SV event registry, conservation, timing and marking components are reused. Public projections identify withheld content and have their own manifest. Hash chaining requires an independently retained checkpoint; it does not alone prove authorship, completeness or resistance to a complete rewrite.

The environment record must identify physical or virtual resources, firmware and operating-system versions, source revision, compiler, dependencies, image hashes, setup and recovery recipe, network perimeter, observation coverage, controls and uncertainty. Physical CPU results are not generalized to other CPU families, GPUs, TPUs or NPUs. A virtual machine only supports claims about properties it actually reproduces. The virtual firmware proposal remains separate and does not change the twenty-five parameters or the laptop firmware.

Both provider API connections must be independently identified, concurrently checked and observed throughout the shared work interval. Authentication, catalogue availability, inference admission and response progress are different states. One unavailable or unobserved required connection prevents a valid start or triggers the received incident policy; it never creates a candidate defeat. The document does not certify that real connections have been tested.

Public questions and [booklets](../BOOKLETS.md) are available. Solutions, marking material and reserved variants remain withheld while needed to protect the open examination. Candidate answers that disclose solutions are not published prematurely. A third party must be able to distinguish documentary audit, computational replay of retained originals and experimental reproduction; unavailable material is an explicit limit. Repeating a remote model call is not guaranteed to reproduce its original output byte for byte.

The Arbiter–Director must govern authorized execution and the transition between examinations. The automated sequence still requires integrated validation with two consecutive synthetic examinations and a different contract; published folders do not prove that implementation.
