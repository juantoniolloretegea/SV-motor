## Formats and accreditation

Kimi K3.1 Pro's booklet and consultation documents use Markdown until applicable evidence establishes other format capabilities. Missing accreditation does not establish general incapacity. A demonstrated documentary capability is reused within its original scope; a change of domain alone does not require repeating it. Results from a historical Kimi version are not transferred to a different version based on a similar name.

Mode, format, domain aptitude, Attacker profile (PA), Defender profile (PD) and permissions are separate dimensions. The historical Markdown-adapted category and the current minimum Markdown requirement have different contracts. Reading three formats in two domains does not establish equal practical aptitude in both.

## Publication and reservation

Solutions, grading keys, reference programs, reserved variants and responses are excluded. They remain withheld until all candidates have finished and closure and disclosure are agreed. Public examples will not be presented as unseen variants for later participants.

The complete technical library and definitive Q01–Q25 manifest remain pending. Before an attempt, fix complete originals, usage rights, question associations, timing and context capacity. Links and undeclared summaries do not replace missing contents.
